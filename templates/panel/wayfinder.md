## Specialists (repo convention)

- **Research tickets about the organisation's own systems** (existing endpoints, other repositories, internal knowledge bases): use the `internal-researcher` agent instead of a generic research subagent. It reads the sources listed in `docs/agents/internal-sources.md`.
- **Before declaring the destination clear** (the map is ready to become a spec): run the challenge panel — launch `plan-challenger` three times in parallel, in the foreground (wait for all three), with the angles `product`, `technical` and `simplicity` on the map's Decisions-so-far. Turn every unresolved objection into a new ticket (`grilling` for a decision, `research` for a missing fact) instead of hiding it.
